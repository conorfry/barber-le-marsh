Everything you need to know about Barber le Marsh

We're at 20 High Street, Burgh le Marsh, Lincolnshire, PE24 5JS — in the centre of the village. Easy to reach from Skegness, Alford, Spilsby and surrounding areas.
Walk-ins are always welcome, subject to availability. To guarantee your slot — especially on Saturdays and during school holidays — you can book online through Fresha.
Prices start from £7 for a Single Length All Over. A Short Back & Sides is £14, a Style Cut is £16, and a Style Cut & Beard Trim bundle is £23. Kids cuts and OAP (67+) cuts are both £12. Beard trims start from £6.
We offer a full range of men's and boys' barbershop services: skin fades, tapers, mullets, flow cuts, crops, buzz fades, afro cuts, scissor cuts, short back & sides, single length cuts, beard trims, hot towel razor line-ups, kids cuts and OAP cuts. We also offer a bundle deal combining a style cut with a beard trim.
A skin fade is where the hair on the back and sides is tapered down to the skin, creating a seamless blend from short to shorter. It can be paired with any style on top — a textured crop, curls, a longer cut or anything else. Included in our Style Cut at £16.
